Privacy Statement & Consent

Please review the data handling policies below to proceed.

1. Introduction

Welcome to the Vodafone Workforce Management (WFM) Portal. We are committed to protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information.

2. Data Collection

We collect information necessary for workforce planning, including but not limited to: Employee ID, Name, Role Allocations, Skills Matrix, and Project Assignments. This data is sourced from internal HR systems (SuccessFactors) and direct input.

3. Purpose of Processing

Your data is processed strictly for the purpose of operational resource management, capacity planning, and project billing. We do not use this data for external marketing purposes.

4. Data Retention

Data is retained in accordance with Vodafone's global data retention policies. Records of project assignments are kept for 5 years for audit compliance.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, rectify, or request erasure of your personal data within the constraints of employment law. Please contact the Data Privacy Officer for specific requests.
